By submitting an enquiry, demo request or other information through this website, you confirm that the information provided is accurate and that AR Solutions may use it to respond to your request.

1. Information We Collect

AR Solutions may collect information that you voluntarily provide through contact forms, demo requests, email, telephone, WhatsApp or other communication channels.

This information may include:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Company or organisation name.
  • Service or product requirements.
  • Project information, messages and supporting documents.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, IP address and website usage data.

Please do not submit confidential, financial, medical, identity or other sensitive information unless it is genuinely required and specifically requested through an agreed secure method.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • Understand your project or service requirements.
  • Prepare quotations, proposals and demonstrations.
  • Provide websites, software, portfolio or career services.
  • Provide technical support and project communication.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, fraud and security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

AR Solutions will not sell your personal information to unrelated third parties.

3. Basis for Processing

Depending on the situation, information may be processed because:

  • You have provided consent.
  • Processing is needed to respond to your enquiry or prepare a requested quotation.
  • Processing is needed to perform an agreed service or contract.
  • Processing is reasonably necessary for security, administration or legitimate business operations.
  • Processing is required to comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared only when reasonably required with service providers that support website hosting, email delivery, analytics, cloud services, payment processing, deployment or technical operations.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, court order, regulatory authority, fraud investigation or protection of legal rights.

Service providers may process information in other countries. Reasonable steps will be taken to use reputable providers and appropriate security measures.

5. Storage and Security

Enquiries may be stored in the website database, email systems, cloud platforms, project files or authorised business records.

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for protecting passwords, login details and confidential information shared through their own devices or accounts.

6. Information Retention

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for enquiries, project delivery, customer support, accounting, dispute resolution, security or legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted, anonymised or securely archived.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and valid exceptions, you may request:

  • Information about the personal data held about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion of information that is no longer required.
  • Restriction or objection to certain processing.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.

AR Solutions may request verification before processing a privacy request. Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, contractual, accounting or security reasons.

8. Cookies, Analytics and External Links

This website may use essential cookies or similar technologies for security, preferences and basic operation.

Analytics services may be introduced to understand visitor numbers, page usage, device types and website performance. This policy will be updated if additional tracking or advertising technologies are introduced.

The website may contain links to third-party websites, social networks or communication platforms. AR Solutions is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external services.

9. Children’s Information

This website and its commercial services are not intentionally directed at children. Personal information relating to a child should not be submitted without the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when services, legal requirements, hosting providers or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact Us

Questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy may be sent through the Contact page.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains the general cancellation and refund approach for customised digital services supplied by AR Solutions.

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}
Most AR Solutions services are customised and involve reserved time, planning and development work. Refund eligibility therefore depends on the work already completed, third-party costs and the terms of the accepted quotation.

1. Project-Specific Terms

The accepted quotation, invoice or written agreement may state specific deposit, milestone, cancellation and refund terms. Where project-specific terms differ from this general policy, the project-specific written terms will apply, subject to applicable law.

2. Advance Payments and Deposits

An advance payment may be required to reserve development time and begin planning, design, research, setup or customisation.

Once work has started, the advance may be used against work completed, administrative time, reserved capacity and committed third-party costs.

3. Cancellation Before Work Starts

A cancellation request made before work begins may be eligible for a refund, less any:

  • Payment-processing or bank charges.
  • Consultation or planning work already completed.
  • Non-refundable third-party purchases.
  • Administrative costs stated in the quotation.

4. Cancellation After Work Starts

When cancellation is requested after work has begun, AR Solutions may calculate the value of:

  • Discovery, consultation and requirement analysis.
  • Design concepts and revisions.
  • Development or content work completed.
  • Project management and communication time.
  • Purchased domains, hosting, plugins or services.
  • Other committed or non-recoverable expenses.

Any eligible remaining balance may be refunded after deducting these amounts.

5. Completed or Delivered Work

Payments for completed, approved, delivered, deployed or downloaded customised work are generally not refundable, except where required by applicable law or expressly agreed in writing.

This may include websites, portfolios, software modules, source files, CVs, cover letters, content and technical services.

6. Software and Custom Development

Software projects may be divided into milestones. Payment for an approved or completed milestone is generally non-refundable.

A cancellation does not automatically provide rights to unfinished source code, internal tools or unlicensed components. Delivery of partially completed work will depend on payment status, technical feasibility and the project agreement.

7. CV, Cover Letter and Content Services

Once writing, editing, research or formatting has started, the relevant payment may become partly or fully non-refundable, depending on the work completed.

Dissatisfaction based solely on a job application outcome, interview result or employer decision does not create automatic refund eligibility.

8. Website, Hosting and Domain Expenses

Domain registrations, hosting plans, licences, paid plugins, certificates, platform fees and similar third-party charges may be non-refundable because they are purchased from external providers.

9. Customer Delays or Abandonment

A project may be considered inactive when the customer does not provide required content, feedback, approvals or payment for an extended period.

Restarting an inactive project may require a revised schedule, reactivation fee or updated quotation. Payments already applied to completed work or reserved capacity may not be refundable.

10. Service Issues and Corrections

Customers should report genuine defects or failures within the support or review period stated in the agreement.

AR Solutions should normally be given a reasonable opportunity to investigate and correct an issue before a refund is considered.

Refunds may not be available for issues caused by unauthorised modifications, customer misuse, third-party outages, expired services or circumstances outside reasonable control.

11. Refund Method and Processing

An approved refund will normally be returned using an agreed method. Processing time may depend on banks, payment processors and service providers.

Any bank charges, currency-conversion differences or processing fees may be deducted where permitted and applicable.

12. How to Request Cancellation or Refund

A request should be submitted in writing and should include:

  • Customer name and contact details.
  • Quotation, invoice or project reference.
  • The reason for cancellation or refund request.
  • Relevant supporting information.

Each request will be reviewed according to the project stage, completed work, third-party expenses, written agreement and applicable law.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Support Questions

Support availability and scope depend on the project and agreed service terms.

Is support included after delivery?

Initial support may be included based on the project agreement. Extended or ongoing support can be arranged separately.

Can new features be added later?

Yes. Websites and software can be expanded when new requirements arise.

Do you provide user training?

Basic or detailed training can be provided depending on the product and agreement.

Can you support a website or system created elsewhere?

It may be possible after reviewing the technology, files, access and current condition of the project.

How quickly will a request be handled?

Response and resolution time depend on the urgency, complexity and current support workload.

Is support free?

Support included in an existing agreement may be free. Additional work may require a separate quotation.
